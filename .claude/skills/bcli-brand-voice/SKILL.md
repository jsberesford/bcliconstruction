# BCLI Brand Voice

Use this skill whenever you are writing or editing any user-facing copy for the BCLI Construction site. This includes hero headlines, section intros, service descriptions, CTAs, captions, alt text, button labels, and metadata.

## Brand identity

- Name: **BCLI Construction & Engineering**.
- Tagline: **"Building Guyana from the Ground Up"**. Never "Shaping Guyana." Never "Transforming Guyana." Use the exact tagline.

## Punctuation rules

- **No em dashes anywhere.** Not in headings, not in body copy, not in captions. Replace with commas, periods, parentheses, or colons depending on the rhythm the sentence needs.
- **No tricolons.** A tricolon is a list of three balanced clauses, usually rhetorically polished ("we plan, we build, we deliver"). Break the rhythm: use two items, four items, or vary the clause lengths so it does not read as patterned.

## Banned vocabulary and phrases

Never use any of the following:

- delve, dive into, let's explore, let's dive in
- navigate, navigating the landscape, the landscape of
- in today's fast-paced world, in today's
- ultimately, in conclusion, to summarize
- it's important to note, it should be noted, worth noting
- unlock, elevate, transform, revolutionize, empower
- pinnacle, excellence, world-class, cutting-edge, best-in-class
- seamless, seamlessly, leverage, robust, holistic, synergy
- personalized consultation, tailored solution, bespoke approach

## Voice characteristics

- Confident, specific, declarative.
- Sentence length is mid-length on average, with deliberate variation. Avoid stacking sentences of the same length next to each other.
- Reference concrete things: years of experience, real materials, real regions, real client outcomes. Specific beats abstract every time.
- No over-polished closing sentences that summarize the obvious or add a motivational flourish. The last line of a section should carry information, not vibe.

## Good example

> Build with engineers who have done this for two decades.

## Bad example (do not write copy like this)

> Experience the pinnacle of construction excellence by scheduling your personalized consultation.

## The Leta bio rule (sacred)

Leta's bio is non-negotiable. Whenever Leta is described, the following details must appear, in full, never abridged or generalized:

- Her career in the **New York garment industry**.
- Her work in **global sourcing**.
- Her role with the **NY Women Chamber of Commerce**.

Do not paraphrase these into vaguer terms ("a long career in fashion," "international business experience"). Use the specifics every time.

## Before finalizing copy

Re-read the copy. If any banned word, em dash, tricolon, or polished-closing pattern is present, rewrite before returning the copy to the user or committing it.
